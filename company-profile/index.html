About AERA

AERA Meal Prep cooks portion-weighed, macro-counted meals for people who train, in Klang Valley, Malaysia.

We have been doing it for 6 years, from our own kitchen at No. 35, Jalan Equine 9A, Taman Equine, Seri Kembangan.

Our meals are built for two goals, Fat Loss and Mass Gain, and every box states its calories, protein, carbs and fat. Customers order on aeramealprep.net or in the AERA app, and we cook, pack and deliver to their door.

AERA Meal Preparation Sdn. Bhd. runs the kitchen, the website and the apps itself, so a plan, a gym fridge or a company order is handled end to end by one team.

PACKAGING

MAP Bento Box Technology

Every AERA meal is packed in a MAP Bento Box. MAP stands for Modified Atmosphere Packaging : before the box is sealed, the air inside is drawn out and replaced with a food-grade gas mix of 70% nitrogen and 30% carbon dioxide.

MAP SEALINGLIVE

Less oxygen in the box means the meal stays the way the kitchen cooked it, for longer.

Nitrogen ( N₂ ) 70%CO₂ 30%
  • Less Oxygen, Slower SpoilageOxygen is what lets food go stale and lets most spoilage bacteria and mould grow. Drawing it out of the box slows both down.
  • Nitrogen ( 70% )An inert gas that already makes up most of the air we breathe. It fills the space the oxygen leaves, keeps the film off the food and protects colour and flavour.
  • Carbon Dioxide ( 30% )Holds back the growth of bacteria and mould on the surface of the food.
  • Sealed Until You Open ItThe film is heat-sealed over the tray, so nobody touches the meal between our kitchen and the customer.
  • Chiller, Not FreezerMAP works together with cold. Kept in the chiller ( 0 – 4 °C ), meals last up to 7 days, and the expiry date is printed on every box.
  • Three CompartmentsProtein, carbs and vegetables sit apart in the tray until it is time to eat. Reheat in the microwave, as shown on the label.
  1. 1Fill

    The weighed portions go into the three compartments of the bento tray.

  2. 2Flush

    The air is drawn out and replaced with 70% nitrogen and 30% carbon dioxide.

  3. 3Seal and Label

    The film is heat-sealed on, and the box gets its sticker with the meal, macros and expiry date.
