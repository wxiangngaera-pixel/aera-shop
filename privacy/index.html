This notice explains how AERA Meal Preparation Sdn. Bhd. collects, uses and protects your personal data, as required by the Personal Data Protection Act 2010. It applies to our website and ordering pages, your AERA account, and orders placed with us by WhatsApp or email.

2. The Personal Data We Collect

  • Contact details : your name, email address, phone number and delivery addresses.
  • Account details : your date of birth and your password. Your password is stored in encrypted form by our account provider; we cannot see it.
  • Orders : what you order, delivery or pick-up date and time, courier, packaging, notes for the kitchen and rider, the amount paid and payment status. Your full card details are handled by our payment providers and never reach us.
  • Food preferences : meal preferences, foods you do not want, allergies, and salt and spice preferences.
  • Meal plan details : gender, age, height, bodyweight, activity level, goal, body type and food you eat outside AERA, used to work out your calorie and macro targets.
  • Weight Control records : bodyweight, body fat, muscle mass, BMI, visceral fat and any body-composition reports you choose to upload.
  • Loyalty : your AERA Points, vouchers, AERA Credit and any referral code you used.
  • Referrers only : the bank details you give us so we can pay your cash-back.
  • Messages : what you send us by email, WhatsApp or chat.
  • On your device : our pages save a few settings in your own browser, such as your language and the checkout details you last used, so you do not have to type them again. You can clear these by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Health-Related Data

Your Weight Control records, body measurements and allergies can reveal information about your physical health, which the law treats as sensitive personal data. We only collect it with your explicit consent, when you choose to enter it. It is optional: you can order without it, and you can delete it from your account at any time.

4. Where We Get It

  • From you, when you order, create an account, build a meal plan or contact us.
  • From our previous website, if you had an account there: your contact details, your last order and your AERA Points balance were carried over to your new account.
  • From our payment providers and couriers, who tell us whether a payment succeeded and how a delivery is progressing.

5. Why We Use It

  • To take, cook, pack and deliver your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To run your account, AERA Points, vouchers, AERA Credit and the Birthday Treat.
  • To build your personalised meal plan and show your progress.
  • To pay referral cash-back and show referrers the orders placed with their code.
  • To answer your questions and complaints, and to put things right.
  • To keep business, accounting and tax records, and to prevent fraud and misuse.
  • If you agree, to send you news and offers. You can stop these at any time.

Your name, contact details, delivery address and payment are needed to fulfil an order; without them we cannot accept it. Everything else is optional.

7. Transfers Outside Malaysia

Some of the providers above store or process data outside Malaysia, for example in Japan and the United States. We use them because they are needed to take and deliver your order, or, for the meal-plan coach, because you chose to use it. We choose established providers that protect personal data, and we send them only what they need.

8. How Long We Keep It

  • Order and payment records : 7 years, as company and tax law requires.
  • Your account, preferences, meal plans and Weight Control records : while your account is open. If you ask us to close it, we delete them within 30 days, apart from the records above.
  • Marketing : until you ask us to stop.

9. How We Protect It

Your account data is protected so that each customer can see only their own. Access to our systems is limited to the people who need it to run AERA. Card payments are handled entirely by our payment providers. If a personal data breach happens that is likely to cause you significant harm, we will tell you and the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

10. Your Rights

  • See the personal data we hold about you, and correct it if it is wrong. Most of it you can view and change yourself under My Account.
  • Withdraw your consent to optional uses, such as health-related data or marketing.
  • Ask us to stop sending you marketing. We only send it if you ticked the box to receive it, and you can switch it off yourself under My Account → Meal Preferences → News & Offers.
  • Ask us to send your data to you or another organisation, where this is technically possible.
  • Complain to us, or to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner Malaysia.

Email your request to aera.mealprep@gmail.com. We will reply within 21 days and may need to confirm your identity first.

11. Children

Our shop is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

12. Changes To This Notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will tell you of any important change by email or on our website.
