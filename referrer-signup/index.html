Questions

When do I get paid?

After each month closes. We total every Order placed with your Code, transfer the Cash-Back to your Bank Account, and stamp the Receipt in your Portal so you can download it.

What counts towards my Cash-Back?

The Meal Subtotal after any Discount the customer used. Delivery, Packaging and Processing Fees are never counted.

Can someone change to another Referrer later?

No. The first paid order ties that customer to your Code for good, so you keep earning from them.

What does the Customer get?

An extra 3% of their Meal Subtotal in AERA Points, on top of the usual 5 AERA Points per RM 1. 100 AERA Points is RM 1.00 OFF a future order.

How do I see my figures?

The Referrer Portal. Enter the Email on your application, we send a Code, and you are in. Sales by month, which Customers ordered, what is pending and what has been paid.
