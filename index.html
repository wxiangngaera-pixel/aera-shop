Packaging & delivery

Fresh, Not Frozen

RM 1.80 per box · Choose at Checkout

MAP Bento Box

Oxygen is removed from the box and replaced with a 70% Nitrogen / 30% CO₂ blend before it is top-sealed. The food is never compressed, so texture stays as the Chef intended. Keep in the chiller — 7-Day shelf life.

RM 1.80 per box · Choose at Checkout

Vacuum Packed

Same freshness, flatter pack. Takes far less fridge space but compresses the food slightly. Good for taking several days of Meals home from the Gym.

Questions

Good to Know

How long do the meals last?

7 Days in the chiller from the day they are packed. The Pack Date is printed on every box. Don't freeze MAP Bento Boxes; it defeats the Packaging. Vacuum Packed can be frozen if you need longer.

How do I reheat?

Peel back the top seal, pour the Sauce Sachet over the food if one is included, and microwave 2 minutes or 3 minutes. A steamer works too.

Allergies and Preferences?

Each Meal lists its components. Meals containing Prawn, Seafood, Dairy, Peanut or Beef are named as such. For No-Spice, Less-Salt or Oil-Free requests, or to exclude an Ingredient entirely, use the Personalised Meal Plan on aeramealprep.net, or message us on WhatsApp before ordering.

Where do you deliver?

Across Klang Valley — Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Subang, Puchong, Seri Kembangan, Cheras, Ampang, Shah Alam and surrounding areas. The Delivery Fee is shown at Checkout based on distance from our Seri Kembangan Kitchen.

What are AERA Points?

Every Order earns 5 AP for each RM 1 spent on Meals ( Packaging and Delivery Fees excluded ). 100 AP = RM 1.00 OFF a future order — Up to 10% of that Order OR towards AERA Merchandise. Points are tied to the Email you order with and expire 6 Months after they are earned. No Sign-In needed : At Checkout, type your email and your balance appears with a dropdown to pick how much to take off before you pay.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Visa, Mastercard, FPX Online Banking and GrabPay are accepted; we never see or store your card details.

Can I change or cancel an order?

We prep your Meals over the 3 days before your slot, so changes and cancellations are possible up to 1 PM, 3 days before Delivery, not counting Sundays. For a Monday delivery, that is 1 PM on the Thursday before. Message us on WhatsApp with your Order Number.
